GAMEPLAY PROGRAMMING / UNREAL ENGINE 5 / BLUEPRINTS

Prototype CRPG

Recreating the foundations of click-to-move navigation inspired by Baldur’s Gate 3.

This personal prototype explores click-to-move, pathfinding with NavMesh, following a curved path built with a Spline, and a camera independent of the character. Basic gathering, inventory, and crafting systems round out the experiment.

02 / NAVIGATION

Movement that follows the chosen path.

The character moves exclusively by clicking. Once the player selects a destination, the NavMesh finds a path around obstacles. A spline creates a curved path for the movement logic to follow.

03 / CAMERA SYSTEMS

Look around freely, then return to the character.

The camera runs in a dedicated Blueprint. The ZQSD keys move the camera independently of the character. A button resumes tracking. The system includes rotation, zoom, and two projection modes.

04 / ADDITIONAL SYSTEMS

Basic interactions expand the prototype.

Alongside navigation, I experimented with click interactions for objects and characters, resource gathering, viewing resources in an inventory, and basic crafting. These features work within the limited scope of the prototype.

06 / TECHNICAL REFLECTION

A working prototype with known limitations.

Movement follows the reconstructed route correctly. The main challenge was balancing path curvature, precision and performance.

Movement logic is concentrated in the Character Blueprint. This organization emerged during prototyping. With what I know today, I would distribute responsibilities across systems.
