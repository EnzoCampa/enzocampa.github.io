TECHNICAL BREAKDOWN / Vorace

Designing the system.

A look at the problems, architectural decisions, and limitations encountered. Blueprint screenshots can be added to each section.

OverviewTechnical breakdown

01 / STRUCTURE

AI Controller and StateTree

The AI Controller drives transitions between Idle, Chase, and Attack. Each state represents a specific behavior intent.

02 / POSITIONING

EQS and strafing

Lateral positioning uses spatial queries and distance constraints. Wall checks help prevent unsuitable positions.

03 / TUNING

Data Assets

Parameters such as distances and speeds are separate from behavior logic to make tuning and iteration easier.

04 / ITERATION

Dodging and debugging

Dodge prototypes combine detection and probability. The project also required fixes to Pawn references and transition conditions.
