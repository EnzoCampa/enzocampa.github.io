03 / PHILOSOPHY

The process matters as much as the outcome.

Designing a system means understanding the problem, making choices, testing them in practice, and refining them.

01 / ARCHITECTURE

Build a clear structure before adding complexity.

I aim to design modular, maintainable systems with clear responsibilities and well-defined interactions.

02 / ITERATION

Test, analyze, improve.

A working prototype is an opportunity to learn. Tests, setbacks, and refactoring help a system improve.

03 / COLLABORATION

Challenge ideas through discussion.

I value teams where people can discuss and challenge decisions. Working across disciplines improves the result.

04 / VISION & GOALS

Connect gameplay and storytelling.

My interest in narrative design informs my work as a gameplay programmer. I want to build systems that support mechanics while contributing to the story and making the world feel coherent.

Player actions, character behavior, and game rules can all help tell a story.

Long-term goal — found or lead a studio.

I want to develop strong technical expertise, understand the full production process, and work on a variety of projects.

Eventually, I want to found or lead my own game studio, combining technical rigor with a creative vision.
